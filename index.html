How it works

  1. Sound becomes a picture. The mic audio becomes a spectrogram: time across, pitch up, loudness as brightness.
  2. Keep only the stars. Just the loudest points survive, about 18 a second. Background noise rarely beats them, so it works in a noisy room.
  3. Pairs of stars become fingerprints. Each star is paired with the next few: pitch, pitch and the gap between them make one tiny code.
  4. Find the song where the codes line up. Hundreds of codes match one song at the same moment, and that spike is the answer.
Songs it knows
Real Shazam knows millions of songs. This one knows the songs above: today's Apple Music charts (US, UK, India) plus all-time pop, Hindi classics, Bollywood love songs and roadtrip songs. It stores only fingerprints, never the audio; song info and links are from Apple Music. Your microphone audio never leaves your phone.
